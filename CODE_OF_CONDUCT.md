# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Local Forge welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, appearance, ability, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome differing technical perspectives and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and respect boundaries.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project and community.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@lavely.io`. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as possible.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
